# UPS Air Transportation Procurement: Process Redesign

A business process improvement project for UPS's air gateway procurement process, completed by a five-person analyst team in MIST 5750 at the University of Georgia (January to May 2025).

**[View the slide deck (PDF)](./BPMN%20UPS%20Air%20Procurement%20Process%20Slides.pdf)**

## The problem

UPS manages about $500 million in air transportation vendor contracts. When a new air gateway opens, the procurement team issues a request for pricing, evaluates vendor bids, negotiates a contract, and onboards the vendor. We were asked where that process loses time and how to shorten it.

## What we did

We followed the four phases of the business process management cycle:

1. **Identification.** Mapped UPS's value chain and process portfolio, and selected air transportation procurement as high in importance and feasible to improve.
2. **Discovery.** Held two interviews with the process owner, reviewed internal request-for-pricing templates and a sample cost model, and modeled the current process in BPMN.
3. **Analysis.** Ran a value-added analysis and a cycle-time analysis on the current process.
4. **Redesign.** Built a new process model and re-ran the cycle-time analysis on it.

## What we found

| Measure | Current process |
| --- | --- |
| Average cycle time | 39.3 days |
| Time spent selecting a vendor | Nearly 14 days, against 1.5 days of actual work |
| Time in contract negotiation and rework | Over 16 days |
| Chance a contract needs rework | 57% |
| Process cycle efficiency | About 44% |

Contract negotiation was the largest gap: its elapsed time was nearly double its working time, and each round of rework added at least 10 days. The causes were unclear contract wording, templates sent before legal sign-off, and last-minute pricing changes.

## What we recommended

- **AI-assisted vendor evaluation.** A tool that scores vendor bids, flags overpriced offers, and suggests the best-fit vendors, replacing a manual review.
- **Pre-approved contract templates.** Standard terms cleared by legal in advance, to cut the back-and-forth in negotiation.

## Projected result

| Measure | Current | Redesigned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average cycle time | 39.3 days | 29.1 days |
| Chance of contract rework | 57% | 30% |

That is a projected 26% reduction in cycle time, or about 10 days per procurement. These figures come from our process model and were not measured in a live rollout.

We also proposed ideas outside the process itself to strengthen vendor relationships, including protective gear, a shuttle program for vendor staff at remote gateways, training support, and performance-based incentives.
